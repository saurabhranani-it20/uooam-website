ABOUT UOOAM

Crafted with
soul and story.

Founded in 2022 by Urvashi Ranani, our atelier is rooted in the timeless artistry of Pushkar, Rajasthan - a place where heritage, craftsmanship, and nature exist in perfect harmony.

We create exclusive hand-painted artisan apparel that celebrates the quiet elegance of the natural world. Inspired by blooming florals, delicate leaves, and organic forms, each design is thoughtfully envisioned and meticulously brought to life by hand. Every garment is individually handcrafted, making each piece a one-of-a-kind expression of wearable art.

We believe true luxury lies in authenticity - the beauty of the handmade, the rarity of individuality, and the stories woven into every creation. Our philosophy preserves traditional craftsmanship while reimagining it for the modern connoisseur. Each piece is designed to transcend seasons and trends, offering timeless elegance infused with soul, heritage, and unparalleled attention to detail.
