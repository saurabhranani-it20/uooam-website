Shipping Policy

We are pleased to offer worldwide shipping on all orders.

Orders are processed within 2-5 business days. Since every piece is handcrafted and hand-painted, processing times may vary during peak seasons or for made-to-order items.

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a tracking link via email or SMS where applicable.

Delivery timelines depend on your location and the courier partner but generally take 3-7 business days after dispatch.

Please ensure that your shipping address and contact details are accurate when placing your order. We are not responsible for delays caused by incorrect shipping information or unforeseen courier delays.
