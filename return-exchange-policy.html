Return & Exchange Policy

At our brand, every garment is individually handcrafted and hand-painted, making each piece unique and created with great care.

Because of the artisanal nature of our products, we do not accept returns or exchanges once an order has been placed and delivered.

We encourage customers to review product descriptions, size guides, and order details carefully before making a purchase. If you have any questions about sizing, colors, or the product, our team will be happy to assist you before you place your order.

In the unlikely event that you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photographs so we can review the issue and provide an appropriate resolution.
